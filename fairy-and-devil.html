# A Fairy and a Devil 🧚‍♀️🦇 Once upon a time, there lived a devil named Damien, the son of a powerful emperor. He belonged to a royal family whose influence extended across a vast kingdom. However, Damien's life was not as ordinary as it appeared. Since the day he was born, a mysterious curse had been placed upon him by his own father. The emperor had placed this curse to protect his village from a terrible fate. According to the curse, Damien would eventually transform into a bloodthirsty vampire. To escape this fate, he had to find his destined bride before his thirty-fourth birthday. If he failed, an incurable and extremely painful disease would take hold of him, and he would die within three months. But Damien knew nothing about this terrible secret during his childhood. When Damien was eight years old, he studied in Class 3 at a school attended by a poor little girl named Lily. Although they attended the same institution, they barely knew each other. One day, during the school's annual day, an unfortunate misunderstanding occurred. Damien was accused of pushing his brother, who had been checking the question papers from Damien's class. Nobody seemed interested in hearing Damien's explanation, and he was severely scolded. Just then, Lily stepped forward. "Damien didn't do it!" she insisted, defending him. Her words helped clear up the misunderstanding, and Damien was finally able to escape the trouble. He was deeply grateful for her kindness. After that incident, the two children gradually became close friends. Damien developed a special affection for Lily. He enjoyed spending time with her, and her presence made his school days more pleasant. Unfortunately, their friendship did not last long. When they reached Class 5, Lily's family faced severe financial difficulties. They had accumulated considerable debt, and her family could no longer manage the expenses of her education. Eventually, Lily had to obtain a Transfer Certificate (TC) and leave the school. She moved to a small, distant village, far away from Damien's city. And so, the two childhood friends were separated. As the years passed, neither of them remembered their childhood friendship. The little girl who had once defended Damien and the boy who had been grateful for her kindness gradually became strangers in each other's lives. Time changed them both. Damien grew into a powerful, courageous and intelligent emperor. His authority was respected throughout the kingdom, and his remarkable leadership made him a formidable ruler. However, beneath his royal appearance, he remained unaware of the terrible fate awaiting him. When Damien turned thirty, his mother finally decided to reveal the truth. On his thirtieth birthday, she told him about the mysterious curse his father had placed upon him. She explained the dreadful transformation awaiting him, the importance of finding his destined bride before his thirty-fourth birthday, and the incurable disease that would claim his life if he failed. Damien was overwhelmed by the revelation. He had always believed that his future belonged to him, but now he discovered that his life was bound by a curse. Nevertheless, he was stubborn. He did not want fate to control his personal decisions, and he refused to willingly accept the idea that someone had already been chosen for him. Meanwhile, Lily's life had taken a completely different direction. She continued her education until Class 8. Because of her family's financial struggles, she eventually began working several jobs to repay their debts. Her daily routine was exhausting. She worked long hours, managed with barely five hours of sleep at night and took a short two-hour afternoon nap before returning to work. Despite her exhaustion, she remained determined to support her family and help them overcome their financial difficulties. One midnight, while returning home, Lily witnessed something alarming. A man was being attacked by a group of boys. She immediately contacted the police for assistance. The boys noticed her and attempted to chase her, but she managed to get help. When the police arrived, the situation was brought under control. Only then did Lily discover the identity of the man she had helped. He was none other than Emperor Damien! Damien's mother was immensely grateful for Lily's courageous act. She thanked the girl and offered her an opportunity to work as Damien's personal maid. She handed Lily a card containing the address of their royal residence. Lily accepted the card, grateful for the unexpected opportunity. Little did she know that this encounter would eventually change the course of both their lives. On her way home, Lily encountered one of her aunts, to whom her family owed ₹60,000. The moment her aunt noticed her, she began questioning her about the unpaid debt. "How long do you expect me to wait? Your family still owes me ₹60,000!" her aunt exclaimed angrily. Lily tried to explain their difficult financial circumstances, but her aunt refused to listen. Instead, she continued humiliating Lily and demanding that the money be returned immediately. Unable to bear the insults any longer, Lily broke into tears. "Please, give me just one more month. I promise I'll try my best to repay the money," she pleaded. Her aunt remained displeased, but Lily had no other choice. With tears in her eyes, she finally returned home. As she entered the house, she looked at the card Damien's mother had given her. It contained the address of the royal palace and the opportunity to work as Damien's personal maid. After thinking about her family's financial difficulties and the debt they needed to repay, Lily made up her mind. She would visit the palace the following morning. The next day, Lily arrived at the royal palace. She was astonished by its magnificent architecture, enormous halls and luxurious surroundings. Everything appeared far beyond the world she had known. Damien's mother welcomed her warmly and arranged everything necessary for her employment. Soon, Lily officially became Damien's personal maid. However, working for the emperor was not going to be easy. Damien was strict, impatient and difficult to please. He preferred his privacy and disliked unnecessary interference in his daily routine. He rarely tolerated mistakes and expected everything to be completed according to his instructions. Lily, on the other hand, remained sincere and determined to perform her responsibilities properly. Although his behaviour sometimes made her work difficult, she continued carrying out her duties. As time passed, their lives became increasingly connected. Damien often found himself helping Lily, even in the most complicated situations, despite sometimes having no desire to get involved. He could be stubborn, but somehow, Lily's troubles repeatedly drew him into situations he would otherwise have avoided. One day, Lily was kidnapped by a group of mysterious men. When Damien discovered her disappearance, he became involved in the situation and began searching for her. Eventually, he managed to rescue her. After a series of unexpected events surrounding the kidnapping, the circumstances led to an engagement between Damien and Lily. Damien was particularly displeased with the situation. He did not believe that fate should control his personal life, nor did he willingly accept the idea that Lily was meant to be his bride. Soon afterwards, a witch arrived and revealed an important secret to Damien. She told him that Lily was his destined bride. Damien later shared this revelation with Lily. As a result, both Lily and his entire family became aware of the truth. However, the witch had another revelation concerning Lily. She explained that Lily was under an additional curse. According to it, Lily could not die until Damien turned forty. No matter how hard anyone tried to kill her, they could hurt her, but they could never take her life before that age. Their destinies were now connected by two separate curses. Meanwhile, Damien's own curse remained connected to his eventual transformation into a bloodthirsty vampire. To remove his curse, he needed to have a child of his own. As time passed, Lily became pregnant. During her pregnancy, she had a personal caretaker who helped look after her. The caretaker's brother gradually became interested in Lily after noticing her beauty. He began offering to cook meals for her and became increasingly involved in her daily life. As they spent more time together, Lily developed an affection for him. Through Lily and the events surrounding her pregnancy, a fairy would eventually appear. The fairy possessed a remarkable ability. Through the baby, she would create a new soul with a special destiny: to protect the kingdom as it grew up. However, this new soul could not become the emperor while Damien was still alive. It would only be able to inherit the throne after Damien's death. After the baby was born, the fairy appeared and created the new soul. While Lily was resting after childbirth, an unexpected betrayal took place. The caretaker's brother took her away. Later, some men demanded Lily and offered him money. Instead of protecting her, he accepted their offer and sold her to them. When Damien discovered what had happened, he went after Lily. He was determined to rescue her, and a fierce confrontation followed. Eventually, Damien managed to save her. However, Lily was injured during the fight. Her condition required considerable time to improve, and she needed six months to recover. By this time, Damien was thirty-eight years old. After Lily's recovery, only one year remained until he turned forty. During this final year, Damien continued struggling with his bloodthirsty nature. He drank blood, and his transformation remained a constant threat. Despite everything that had happened, Damien grew to love Lily deeply. Their lives were approaching a tragic turning point, one that neither could escape. When Damien finally reached forty, Lily's curse could no longer protect her. Lily died. Damien was devastated by her death. The loss left him facing a future he could never have imagined. Eventually, Damien went to war, where he too met his end. The kingdom was left with the new soul created through the fairy and their child. Destined to protect the kingdom, this soul would eventually inherit the throne after Damien's death.
